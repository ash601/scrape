# Scrape

Paste a LinkedIn post link, get everyone who commented: name, headline, profile link and their comment. Download it as a CSV.

## Use it
1. **Download** this folder: green **Code** button → **Download ZIP**, then unzip it.
2. **Double-click** `START.cmd` (Windows) or `START.command` (Mac). It opens in your browser.
3. **Add your Apify key** at the top (free at [apify.com](https://console.apify.com/settings/integrations)).
4. **Paste a post link → Get commenters → Download CSV.**

About $0.20 per 100 comments. Apify's free $5 a month covers ~2,500.

You need: [Node.js](https://nodejs.org). Everything stays on your computer, in the `my-scrapes` folder.
