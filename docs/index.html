Scrape

Paste a LinkedIn post link. Get everyone who commented: name, headline, profile and their comment.

Download free
  1. Unzip it.
  2. Double-click START.cmd (Windows) or START.command (Mac).
  3. Add your free Apify key at the top.
  4. Paste a post link → Get commenters → Download CSV.

Needs Node.js. About $0.20 per 100 comments; Apify free $5 a month covers ~2,500. Code: github.com/ash601/scrape
